## Decision

R53 fails functional, parent-nondegradation, per-capability,
source-noninferiority, source-retention, and zero-collapse gates.  The
disclosed final-test was therefore prohibited.  The shallow post-transformer
capability-cake representation is closed; no nearby rate, loss-weight, or step
sweep is authorized.  Exact labeling and routing worked, while the isolated
late residual lacked sufficient causal authority over generation.  The next
architecture must inject a physically sparse capability path inside the
transformer stack while preserving the shared host.

This result does not establish unrestricted English, global minimality, human
quality, independent hardware, or superiority to LoRA or distillation.  The
ABI moonshot remains **OPEN**.
