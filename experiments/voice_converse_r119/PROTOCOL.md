**Question.** Can an experimental ABI filter recognize the same directed event
expressed in three forms and reject the corresponding three wrong-direction
claims? Historical R118 status-only, rerun on exactly the new inputs, is the
primary control, not its historical percentage. Compare R118 full too. A valid
converse uses a different predicate and the other participant's perspective,
not simply passive voice. Swapping the people must change truth. Give permission
and plans scope over the *specified event*, not over a different person's
personal rights/intent. Do not equate sending with receipt or invitation with
attendance. Use asymmetric, determinate relations; document semantic assumptions.

**Candidate.** Retain the frozen R118 lexical and three-state requirements,
target binding and original output bytes. Replace the brittle role grammar
with a model comparison: consistently substitute the two roster names by
Alice/Bob in bound note and proposal, testing BOTH bijections. For each
assignment score aligned note/proposal in both directions, then exchange the
two people in the proposal alone and repeat. Require strict argmax entailment
on all four aligned comparisons AND a strictly higher entailment margin
(entailment logit minus maximum competing logit) than the corresponding
swapped comparison in all four. Ties, absent people, blanks, alias collisions
or unknown modality fail closed. Alias text is only a scoring view, never
deployed or used as accepted text. Includes an alias-only ablation without the
swap preference; log ordinary one-way NLI, R117 and exact frozen R118
status-only/full. Same pinned NLI for all arms. No test-event dictionaries,
family, voice, role labels, expected ids, teacher scores or gold in method.

**Prospective panel.** Publish registration; tested method freeze *before*
writing the eight evaluation families; actual complete generator, packet and
driver *before* model inference; all live labels *before* any scoring.
Method-commit seed for nonce IDs/order. Eight event families x six project
pairs x 1/2 project notes x authorized/planned/occurred = 288 contexts.
Rotate participant assignment independently of alphabetical order. For each:
three authored positives (active, passive, converse) and their exact matched
name transpositions (identical word multiset, no other changes); two wrong
states, one joint status+role foil, mixed supported+unsupported assertion,
omission, wrong project; one live free response. Two no-notes controls per
cluster = 96. Thus 3,840 rows/arm including 864 paired positives and 864
pure swaps. Each method receives only text, requested project, notes and an
alphabetical, untyped two-name roster. No inspection of model scores to
select examples, threshold tuning, retries, fallback rewrites or repairs.

**Unchanged gates.** >=44/48 complete clusters AND >=5/6 in each family.

delta lower bound >= -0.05 and invalid-admission delta upper bound <0.
