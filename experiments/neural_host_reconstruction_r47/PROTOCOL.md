# R47 preregistration: neural LayerCake host reconstruction

R46 falsified the hypothesis that the 7.18M Phase-2 sparse core can acquire
autonomous English from 2.8M labeled teacher tokens by naive causal
fine-tuning.  R47 makes one materially different, evidence-supported move: it
reconstructs the previously measured 82.9M six-block LayerCake neural host from
the still-immutable LayerCake parents and replays the earlier bounded
acquisition.  This is checkpoint recovery and causal-neural feasibility, not a
new nearby sweep.

## Frozen training

- seed 9824;
- 3,000 successful optimizer steps, batch size 8;
- balanced capability sampling;
- all 82,918,666 parameters trainable;
- shared learning rate 2e-5 and cake learning rate 1e-4;
- weight decay 0.01, classifier weight 0.25, prompt-overlap weight 1.0;
- maximum sequence 256;
- autonomous-prefix recovery begins at step 400, every eight steps, with
  horizons 8, 16, and 32;
- CUDA training; and
- no source teacher, logits, hidden activations, planner, or symbolic surface
  handler at training or inference.

## Development reconstruction gates

The reconstruction passes only if:

1. all frozen inputs and the rebuilt base hash exactly match;
2. 1,400 stored validation prompts are regenerated by the neural model;
3. functional accuracy is at least 0.88 overall and 0.60 in every capability;
4. candidate-minus-source functional accuracy is not negative;
5. no more than two collapse cases occur under the frozen lexical-truncate-4
   decoding contract;
6. routing is exact on every row and only one task cake is active;
7. the teacher is absent and no teacher parameters, logits, or activations are
   retained; and
8. a strict verifier recomputes every raw row and fails closed.

A pass authorizes a genuinely fresh prospective teacher-relative replication
and information-minimization ladder.  It does not alone prove unrestricted
English, global minimality, LoRA/distillation superiority, or the full ABI
moonshot.
