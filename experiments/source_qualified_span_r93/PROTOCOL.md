# R93 preregistration: source-qualified prospective structural span

R92 was never a candidate test: the pinned source failed its prerequisite and
the R91 candidate was not loaded. R92 nevertheless supplied a blinded
source-interface search over seven syntactic families. Four families reached
at least 189/200 source accuracy (R92 families 1, 3, 5, and 6); the other three
are rejected for this campaign.

R93 freezes that source-only selection rule, then creates a new 1,400-row
catalog using the four selected relation forms with new prompts, five unseen
outer interfaces, new triples, a new subject prefix, new seven-digit
identities, independent semantic-role rotations, candidate-order rotations,
and balanced candidate-list placements. The R91 candidate has never executed
any selected R92 or R93 row. Exact prompt overlap with all earlier catalogs is
prohibited.

The ordered, fail-closed procedure mirrors R92: commit code; materialize,
verify, hash, and commit the catalog; qualify the pinned source before any
candidate access; bind the unchanged R91 candidate, source evidence, catalog,
and implementations; commit the binding; then execute the candidate, parent,
and deterministic random bridge.

Source requires at least 1,330/1,400 prior-corrected selections, zero corrected
ties, and representation of every display position. Candidate gates are
1,330/1,400 overall, 90% in each of four selected syntax families, 95% source
retention, at least +50 percentage points over the frozen parent and random
bridge, random no higher than 500/1,400, zero collapse, physical sparse traces
on all rows, teacher absence, and unchanged bound artifacts.

A pass certifies only bounded prospective transfer on a teacher-qualified
two-hop reasoning interface. It does not certify broad English, arbitrary
domains, global minimality, LoRA/distillation superiority, or the full ABI
moonshot.
