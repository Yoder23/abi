# R76/R77 protocol: falsify the fixed-surface shortcut

R74 failed at 0/700 while emitting training-era `C...` forms against disjoint
`F...` targets. Its source passed 692/700 and untouched parent passed 38/700.
The negative evidence is sealed at commit `fec9f9a`.

R76 changes only the extracted training substrate. The LayerCake parent,
architecture, sparse runtime, broad-English anchor, optimizer family, and
autonomous-generation evaluation remain fixed. Across 8,400 nonce prompts,
the lexical stems assigned to semantic roles rotate and the destination's
position in the displayed candidate list varies. No stem used by prospective
R77 validation appears in R76 training.

R77 is generated and committed before source scoring, artifact construction,
or candidate training. It has 1,400 rows, 200 per frozen premise family,
disjoint stems, subject prefix, nonce range, and prompt strings. Source and
candidate gates are at least 95% overall, at least 90% in every family, no
ties in source conditional scoring, zero candidate collapse, at least 95%
retention of source-passing rows, and at least a 50 percentage-point causal
gain over the untouched R47 parent. Inference must execute only six adapters
and one terminal cake for the reasoning route, with the source absent.

This can prove only bounded role-invariant capability acquisition. It cannot
certify unrestricted English, domain transfer, minimization, independent
hardware, human preference, or the full ABI moonshot.
