candidate gates are at least 95% overall, at least 90% in every family, no

and one terminal cake for the reasoning route, with the source absent.

This can prove only bounded role-invariant capability acquisition. It cannot
certify unrestricted English, domain transfer, minimization, independent
hardware, human preference, or the full ABI moonshot.
