# R19 disclosed-development contrastive-realization protocol

R19 is a materially different successor to failed R18. Both R17-v2 public
evidence and the failed R18 hidden evidence are disclosed development sets;
neither is represented as a new holdout.

The compiler learns slot templates from free teacher outputs. For questions
and future declaratives, it jointly selects positive/negative templates at the
same mood, tense, and subject number when the negative surface differs by one
additional literal token. The literal token itself is learned and is not
hard-coded. Other signatures use local empirical consensus. No cross-number
pooling is permitted.

The physical compiler receives only 72 anonymous extraction IDs, signatures,
slots, and outputs. It receives no prompts, expected outputs, evaluation rows,
source model, secret, reveal, or success IDs and runs in a Linux
pivot-root/no-network capsule.

The development prerequisite passes only if both disclosed datasets produce
24-template packages that score 48/48 functional evaluation, regress on zero
source-correct rows, match or beat independent modal consensus, abstain 48/48
when removed, and score at most 10% under mood permutation. Passing authorizes
only a fresh preregistered hidden lexical replication.

No development or hidden result proves unrestricted English, prompt
understanding, conversation, reasoning, autonomous labeling, arbitrary-domain
extraction, production LayerCake ingestion, minimality, or superiority to
LoRA or distillation.
