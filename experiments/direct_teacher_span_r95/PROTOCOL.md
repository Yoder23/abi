# R95 preregistration: direct paired teacher-transfer test

R92 through R94 established that requiring the frozen teacher to score 95%
before permitting a candidate comparison is a brittle prerequisite, not a
test of transfer. R95 replaces it on an entirely fresh prospective surface.
It does not rescore or promote any prior campaign.

The frozen source is executed first and its native three-choice conditional
likelihood rows are captured immutably. Source authorization is limited to
capture integrity: all 1,400 rows, catalog binding, every answer display
position, and zero native ties. No source quality threshold is used. The
source result and raw rows must be committed before a candidate binding can
exist, and that binding must be committed before candidate execution.

The unchanged R91 LayerCake candidate must pass at least 1,330/1,400, at
least 315/350 in every structural family, retain at least 95% of source-correct
rows, equal or exceed the source point score, and have a paired bootstrap 95%
lower confidence bound of at least -0.02 relative to the source. It must also
beat the frozen parent and randomized bridge by at least 50 percentage points,
keep random performance at or below 500/1,400, show no collapse, execute the
registered sparse path on every row, retain no source model, and preserve all
bound hashes.

R95 has 1,400 new prompts with fresh wrappers, nonce labels, subjects, numeric
identities, and balanced candidate placement. R91 has never observed an R95
candidate row. Passing would prove bounded prospective teacher-derived
two-hop reasoning transfer under a direct paired comparison. It would not
prove broad English, arbitrary domains, global minimality, superiority to
LoRA or distillation, human quality, independent hardware, or the full ABI
moonshot.
