# R90 preregistration: broad-surface joint-span diagnostic

R88/R89 established a frozen 530,050-parameter joint-span package on two
prospective bounded nonce-reasoning matrices. R90 tests that exact package,
without retraining or calibration, on the 100 `domain_independent_reasoning`
rows in the already disclosed R60 catalog. This is a cross-surface development
diagnostic, not a promotion test.

The script must bind the R60 catalog and raw evidence, the R88 bridge and
metadata, the unchanged R76 LayerCake host checkpoint and metadata, and the
imported capability artifact. It executes the trained bridge and a newly
initialized deterministic random bridge on every row. The source teacher is
not loaded.

The diagnostic passes only if:

- all 100 registered reasoning rows execute;
- the unchanged R88 package passes at least 95/100;
- it retains at least 95% of R60 source-passing rows;
- it improves by at least 50/100 over the frozen R60 endpoint;
- the random bridge passes no more than 35/100;
- collapse and generation errors are zero; and
- exactly one reasoning cake, six reasoning adapters, and the joint-span
  bridge physically execute per row.

Regardless of outcome, R60 remains a failed prospective endpoint and R90 is
not promotion eligible. A pass authorizes integration of the unchanged R88
package into a materially broader modular English endpoint before a new
prospective catalog. It does not certify broad English or the ABI moonshot.
