# R88 protocol: lexical-invariant joint neural span transfer

R85 established a causal stateful-span effect but failed quality. R86 and R87
then correctly closed before candidate training because a high-quality source
missed a slice-level source floor. Those source screens passed 1,371 and 1,352
of 1,400 overall with zero ties. Because the candidate is trained from a
separate immutable teacher-labeled artifact, a source family likelihood slice
is a ceiling diagnostic, not evidence about candidate transfer. R88 corrects
that category error prospectively: source authorization requires at least
1,330/1,400 overall, all display positions, zero corrected ties, and complete
rows. Source family counts remain mandatory reported diagnostics. Candidate
family floors remain strict and unchanged.

R88 implements the previously untested R86/R87 mechanism. Every optimizer
example is a deterministic bijective lexical renaming of one immutable R76
teacher record, including the response under the same mapping. This adds no
teacher claim or target. A compact bidirectional bridge scores token start/end
pairs jointly, normalizing over every valid span up to 16 tokens. The deployed
runtime contains no rules, prompt templates, output lookup table, source
weights, source logits, or source activations.

Fresh prospective validation is frozen before source scoring or candidate
training: 1,400 distinct rows, 200 in each of seven families, numeric namespace
995000 through 996399, subject prefix `REF`, seven unseen lexical triples, and
zero candidate observations.

One CUDA candidate is authorized after the corrected source gate passes: seed
88001, width 128, two bidirectional Transformer encoder layers, four heads,
feed-forward width 512, maximum prompt 256 tokens, maximum span 16 tokens,
batch 32, 3,000 successful AdamW steps, learning rate 1e-3, and weight decay
0.01. All 8,129 immutable R76 records must be covered. Generated training stems
and numeric values are disjoint from the R88 validation namespace. Only bridge
parameters train.

Before candidate generation, every package, input, implementation, protocol,
and evidence hash is bound. Passing requires at least 1,330/1,400 exact
outputs, at least 180/200 per family, at least 95% retention of source-passing
rows, at least a 0.50 paired gain over the unchanged R78 parent, at least a
0.50 paired gain over a deterministic random bridge, no more than 500 random-
bridge passes, zero collapse, and physical execution of one capability cake,
six deep adapters, and one joint-span bridge.

Passing proves bounded extractive teacher-to-artifact-to-neural-package-to-
LayerCake transfer under unseen identities. It does not prove unrestricted
English, domains, minimality, LoRA/distillation superiority, or the full ABI
moonshot. The full ABI moonshot remains `OPEN`.
