# R28 error-correcting open-label acquisition

R27 v1 failed closed because a mostly-correct source produced one wrong answer
and one synonymous label disagreement. R28 is a materially different
error-correcting compiler: it requires one unique 2-of-3 quorum independently
for semantic answer keys and literal free labels. Ties, three-way disagreement,
invalid labels, and fewer than two agreeing observations fail closed. It does
not contain or consume label aliases, oracle answers, oracle domains, fact IDs,
the reveal, or success IDs.

The disclosed prerequisite replays the already public R27 qualification rows
through the physical R28 compiler. Public V1 is preserved as a harness failure
caused by the old R16 two-namespace router. Public V2 is preserved at 22/24
because two disclosed subject keys did not occur in all paraphrases. Public V3
uses preregistered common lexical stems and passes 24/24. None used hidden R28
facts. The held-out universe contains 24 entirely
new facts, six each in chemistry, geography, mathematics, and Python. A fresh
secret selects three per oracle domain after implementation freeze.

Registered source gates are at least 68/72 strict JSON parses, oracle-correct
answers, and semantically valid free labels; unique answer and label quorums
for all 12 selected facts; 12 compiled facts; at least four pure discovered
labels; 36/36 package-oracle exact outputs; at least 32/36 normalized agreement
with independent evaluation answers; 36/36 other-package abstentions; no more
than 10% oracle accuracy under answer rotation; and zero oracle/alias fields
inside the physical compiler. LayerCake gates remain exact across three builds,
three CPU/GPU hosts, English immutability, removal/restoration, and corruption.

A pass remains bounded open-label segregation over supplied probes. It is not
exhaustive source diagnosis, fluent English extraction, open-world ontology
induction, global minimality, or LoRA/distillation superiority.
