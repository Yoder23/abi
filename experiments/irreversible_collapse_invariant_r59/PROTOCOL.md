# R59 preregistration: irreversible collapse-transition invariant

R56 preemptively masked repeated token four-grams and damaged valid copying.
R57 altered language-model training and lost quality without eliminating
collapse. R58 learned a separate stop decision from teacher-forced states, but
stopped valid responses early and missed autonomous off-manifold loops. Those
results reject prediction or token substitution as the termination mechanism.

R59 freezes the exact R55 model and R53 router. Before accepting a greedy
token, the runtime evaluates only two irreversible conditions already present
in the locked collapse definition: a sixth identical-token run, or a fourth
repeated lexical four-gram not present in the prompt. If accepting the token
would cross either boundary, generation terminates without accepting it. The
runtime never masks, rescales, replaces, retries, rewrites, or selects another
token. It does not act on the reversible low-unique-token-ratio condition.

Because both monitored counts are monotonic, any response that finishes below
the locked collapse boundaries is provably byte-identical to ordinary R55
greedy decoding. A truncated degenerate prefix still has to pass the unchanged
functional evaluator; the invariant cannot manufacture a functional pass.

## Frozen inputs and decision rule

- exact R55 checkpoint and metadata, R53 router, catalog, source evidence,
  broad archive, anchor archive, and canonical ABI hashes remain frozen;
- training steps, learned parameters, and teacher calls: zero;
- thresholds are exactly the locked collapse thresholds (6 and 4), not tuned
  values; and
- the runtime records accepted tokens, rejected boundary tokens, condition
  counts, and verifies exact selected-route execution.

Disclosed validation must reach at least 1,277/1,400, at least 65/100 per
capability, at least 94% source-pass retention, zero collapse/errors, exact
routing, and exact physical sparsity. If it passes, disclosed final-test must
reach at least 1,261/1,400 under the same gates. Neither disclosed split can
promote R59.

Passing both freezes the neural-plus-runtime endpoint and authorizes exactly
one new 100-per-capability prospective catalog and live pinned-source capture,
created after the endpoint and screen code are sealed. That fresh evaluation
must pass every unchanged gate and prove that all naturally non-collapsed
ordinary-greedy trajectories are byte-identical under the invariant.

Failure closes this invariant. No threshold or condition sweep is permitted.
R59 cannot by itself establish unrestricted English, global minimality, human
quality, independent hardware, or LoRA/distillation superiority. The full ABI
moonshot remains open until its separately registered gates pass.
