certificates retained on the curated default branch.

These files are immutable historical evidence. Their internal paths and commit
identifiers refer to the exact campaign tree preserved on
`research-history-v1089`; moving the compact copies here does not rewrite their
contents or expand their claims.

For raw observations, protocols, negative results, large catalogs, and model
artifacts, use the research-history branch. Start with
`ABI_CAPABILITY_COMPILER_CAMPAIGN_STATE_V1089.json` and follow only the paths it
or the relevant certificate names.
