## Sparsity result

Four nested English budgets were preregistered. Budget 3 passed three seeds at
1,400/1,400 per seed using 1,381 records, 39,136 teacher tokens, and 122,311
teacher-output bytes. The next lower budget used 688 records and 19,497 tokens
but failed at 1,381/1,400 with 19 source-passing regressions.

The correct claim is “lowest passing tested budget,” not “globally minimal
English.” The full source survey and the selected domain records are accounted
separately. No teacher text, logits, activations, source parameters, or `.abix`
bundle is deployed.

## What this does not prove

It does not prove exhaustive latent-domain discovery, universal prompt-level
identity, an absolute information minimum, automatic compatibility with every
open-weight model, arbitrary Python/chemistry/civics mastery, or an audit by an
independent organization. Each expansion needs a new catalog, disjoint
evaluation, adjacent budget failure, package certificate, and final
teacher-free LayerCake test.

`LAYERCAKE_ACQUISITION_PROTOCOL.json` is the original preregistered campaign
contract and intentionally retains its historical state. Later frozen
protocols, amendments, and the v2 release certificate close the reference
campaign without rewriting that history.

## Post-certificate falsification

Before applying a completion tag, a 28-prompt novel-form audit was frozen and
run against the exact v47 artifact and frozen Phi-3 source. LayerCake passed
0/28; Phi-3 passed 19/28; all 19 source passes were LayerCake regressions.
Outputs show failures in grammar, coherence, grounding, instruction following,
conversation, summarization, rewriting, email, tone, formatting,
clarification, abstention, simple reasoning, and fluent realization.

This does not alter the historical 1,700-case certificate. It proves that the
catalog was too structurally narrow to support the broader fluency claim.
`ABI_POSTCERT_GENERALIZATION_AUDIT_DECISION.json` is now the controlling
product-level decision. A successor must pass a materially broader
generalization suite before the moonshot can close.
